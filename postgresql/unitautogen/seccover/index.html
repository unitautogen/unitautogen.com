Most PostgreSQL apps that hold more than one customer's data rely on Row-Level Security and careful function logic to keep tenant A from ever seeing tenant B. seccover proves it stays true as the code keeps shipping.

Isolation, judged per routine

seccover exercises your functions, views and triggers as different tenants and reports, per routine, whether isolation is enforced or leaking, with the evidence and the exact tables and channel a leak reaches. It runs entirely on your side, with no telemetry, so nothing about your schema or data leaves your machine. The --pii pass adds a second check that catches personal data escaping through error and RAISE messages, the leak nobody writes a test for.

Evidence, not a certificate

Because seccover emits standard pgTAP and dated, reproducible reports, its output doubles as supporting evidence for controls under HIPAA and SOC 2, produced inside your perimeter. It is a tool that produces evidence, never a certification or an audit service.
